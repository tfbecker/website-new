---
title: "Data Wants to Be Free"
date: "2026-10-11"
type: "rougher-thought"
---

Data wants to be free. News agencies have long struggled to charge for information others can find. LLMs make even proprietary datasets easier to reconstruct. What remains scarce? Trust, truly private data, and history.

Imagine saving App Store or Chrome Web Store data every day. Anyone can scrape today's snapshot. Nobody can go back and collect the changes you recorded last year. That archive grows more valuable simply because time passes.
